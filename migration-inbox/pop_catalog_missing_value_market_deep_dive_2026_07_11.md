# Missing-value market deep dive — 2026-07-11

## Scope and method

- Batch: the 36 cleaned catalog identities supplied in the July 11 handoff.
- Currency: USD.
- Catalog baseline: current in-box/new market value for the exact catalog identity. This is written to `public.pop_catalog.estimated_value`.
- Item-specific value: reserved for a sticker, variant, signature, or condition that differs from the catalog identity. The damaged Praetorian Guard needs condition-specific evidence before `public.user_collection_items.current_value` should be set.
- Apply threshold: `confidence >= 0.75`, consistent with the project rule. Retail asking prices and preorders alone do not clear the threshold.
- PriceCharting figures are based on completed sales and exclude unsold listings. POPs Today evidence was accepted only when the exact identity had recent sold history. Shipping was not added to the baseline.
